TRAY POLICY — USER REVISION, 2026-10-17

Automatic initial boiler fill and automatic idle pressure release require
fresh local gross weight >=150g; uncalibrated/missing/stale/offline fail closed.
Home Flush is manual: bypass tray weight,including while startup fill waits.
Tank,physical switch,temperature and display-contact checks remain. Automated
backflush/descale cycles retain their existing guard. Ordinary espresso/manual
brewing and physical steam/hotwater do not require tray weight.

The complete original Gaggia Classic Pro OEM tray assembly weight could not
be verified from manufacturer/parts sources. Primary aftermarket vendor
Coffee Sensor lists a compatible stainless low-profile tray at200g:
https://coffee-sensor.com/product/gaggia-classic-2019-pro-or-evo-stainless-steel-short-and-low-profile-drip-tray/
75% of that reference is150g. This is a PROVISIONAL USER-REQUESTED ESTIMATE,
not a measured OEM assembly specification; do not label it as the stock mass.
Minimum is editable in Menu > Tray guard. A no-tray raw baseline still needs
to be captured and saved; boot tare is not a valid substitute. Confirm actual
empty installed tray exceeds minimum,then remove it and verify gross drops
below minimum. Cups/containers of sufficient mass also satisfy load detection.

Startup fill's timer begins only after prerequisites are met; losing tray
stops pump/valve and resets the fill budget. It cannot use up its timeout
while waiting for calibration and then enable heat without having filled.
Deduplicated setup notice permits entering the calibration menu.
