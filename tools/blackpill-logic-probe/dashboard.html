Measurement limits and controls

Analog mode records actual ADC voltage samples on PA0 using DMA. Fastest mode has 64 levels (about 52 mV per step); Detail mode has 4,096 levels. The voltage reference is estimated from the chip's factory-calibrated internal reference. This unbuffered ADC loads the source; high-impedance signals may need the Long acquisition preset. Inputs must remain between GND and the spare's 3.3 V supply. No negative voltage or 5 V input.

Each analog window is uniformly sampled without CPU interrupt gaps, then sampling stops for USB transfer. An 8.192 ms window at 4 MS/s is not a continuous stream over USB. Digital mode records HIGH/LOW timing only; its polling gaps are shown. Disconnected inputs float. Overview plots preserve sample minima and maxima; zoom in for individual sample points.

Run live updates at the fastest rate capture and USB transfer allow. Pause freezes the displayed trace immediately; an in-flight capture may finish and enter history. Select History while paused to inspect it. Scroll to zoom and drag to pan. Input checkboxes apply to digital traces. Analog edge alignment uses the selected voltage threshold.
